    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    About this policy

    This policy explains how Aizal Enterprises ("we", "us") handles information in our Android apps and on this website.

    Information we collect

    Most of our apps do not need an account. Data you enter in an app, such as notes, expenses or measurements, stays on your device unless the app clearly offers a backup or sync feature that you choose to use.

    This website does not use cookies for tracking. If you send us a message, we receive the details you include, such as your name, email address and message, and we use them only to reply to you.

    Permissions

    Our apps ask only for permissions needed for a feature to work, for example access to files for saving a backup, or notifications for reminders. You can review and change permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

    Third-party services

    Some apps may use services such as Google Play services, Firebase or Google AdMob. These services may collect device identifiers or usage data under their own policies. Where an app uses them, this is stated on the app's Google Play listing. Google's policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

    Sharing

    We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when the law requires it or when you ask us to.

    Security and retention

    We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Messages sent to us are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

    Children

    Our apps are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy? Write to us at our email.
